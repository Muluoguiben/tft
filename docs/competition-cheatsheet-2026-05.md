# Competition Cheat Sheet

Use this during practice and before tournament games. Do not add new comps mid-block.

## Stage Rules

- 2-1 / 2-5: level only when it protects streak or board quality.
- 2-4: pick Star God reward by board direction, not by fantasy cap.
- 3-2: decide whether you are a fast-8/9 game, Stage 4 stabilizer game, or reroll game.
- 3-4: second Star God choice should support the real direction.
- 4-2: default major roll-down point for level 8 lines.
- 4-4: third Star God choice should strengthen the board you are already playing.
- 4-7: spend only for immediate board improvement or the next roll-down.
- 5+: scout every round; positioning and contest management matter more than guide purity.

## 3-2 Decision Matrix

| Spot | Default Play |
| --- | --- |
| High HP, strong economy, streak intact | Push toward level 8/9; prioritize `新星九五` or high-cost flex |
| Medium HP, decent items, no clear reroll high-roll | Prepare 4-2 level 8 roll-down into `重装妖姬`, `机甲Flex`, or `木灵飞机` |
| Low HP, strong reroll opener, matching items | Commit to `海魔大卑` / `牧羊维克托` style stabilization |
| Forced loss streak with 3 Anima or clean cashout angle | Consider `幻灵阿萝拉`; otherwise do not force it |

## Comp Cards

### 新星九五

- Role: main late-game flex.
- Enter when: economy is strong, HP is high, or opener is winning.
- Core idea: use high-cost carries; physical items can go to Graves/Fiora style units, AP items can support Vex-style cap.
- Roll timing: 4-2 / 4-5 / 4-7 to level 9 depending on HP and economy.
- Abandon when: low HP, weak frontline, no path to level 9, or too many high-cost contesters.
- Notes: strongest as a cap board, not as a low-HP panic line.

### 重装妖姬

- Role: main AP Stage 4 stabilization comp.
- Enter when: AP items are natural and LeBlanc/Karma/Nunu/Illaoi pieces appear.
- Core board: LeBlanc, Karma, Nunu, Illaoi, Meepsie, Mordekaiser, Zoe, Leona.
- Core items: LeBlanc with Guinsoo / Jeweled Gauntlet / Archangel-style AP scaling; Karma with Shojin / Void Staff / AP; Nunu or Illaoi with tank items.
- Star God preference from current guide references: Varus first, then Ekko, Ahri, or Yasuo.
- Roll timing: 4-1 or 4-2 level 8; do not greed interest before key four-cost upgrades.
- Abandon when: no AP items and LeBlanc is heavily contested.

### 木灵飞机

- Role: high-upside 4-cost board.
- Enter when: Woodling opener, economy augment, or early Corki/Rammus access is strong.
- Core idea: 7 Woodling creates enough board value to support 4-cost three-star chase.
- Core items: Corki damage, Rammus tank, Riven secondary physical items.
- Roll timing: 4-2 level 8; stabilize with Corki/Rammus two-star before chasing.
- Abandon when: cannot open Woodling cleanly or HP is too low to chase.

### 机甲Flex / 太空律动转

- Role: fallback Stage 4 lock board.
- Enter when: tank + AP items are available or Space Groove emblem appears.
- Core idea: use Mech/Galio/Aurelion Sol/Karma-style flexible carries, or Space Groove emblem on Riven when available.
- Roll timing: 4-2 level 8; stabilize first, then decide level 9 or 4-cost chase.
- Abandon when: you are missing both frontline and carry items.

### 海魔大卑

- Role: conditional reroll / tempo line.
- Enter when: opener gives Sea Monster/N.O.V.A pieces, items match Bel'Veth/Akali-style carries, and contest is low.
- Roll timing: 3-2 level 6 for two-star core, then slow-roll key three-stars.
- Abandon when: no upgraded carry by mid Stage 3 or multiple players are holding the same core.

### 牧羊维克托

- Role: conditional mid-cost AP line.
- Enter when: Viktor support item / psionic-style setup appears and frontline is not contested.
- Star God preference from current guide references: Varus is preferred.
- Roll timing: 3-5 to 4-1 level 7/8 depending on HP; stabilize first, chase later.
- Abandon when: no Viktor access, no suitable AP items, or stronger AP players are contesting.

### 幻灵阿萝拉

- Role: high-risk weapon, not default.
- Enter when: 3 Anima opener or clear loss-streak cashout before 3-2.
- Current guide references favor Soraka, Varus, and Kayle; avoid forcing with poor Star God support.
- Rule: cash out on a clean threshold and stabilize. Do not chase entertainment value in a points format.

## Scouting Rules

- Count contesters at 3-2, 4-1, and before the main roll-down.
- If two players share your exact core carry and you are not ahead, pivot.
- Position against the strongest remaining player, not the last player you fought.
- In top four, move carry every round unless Zephyr/Shroud-style threats are irrelevant.

## Risk Switch

- Normal game: top four first.
- Behind in event standings: take one higher-cap line only if the opener naturally supports it.
- Last game and must win: allow Woodling three-star chase or Anima cashout.
