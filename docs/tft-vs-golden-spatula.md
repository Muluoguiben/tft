# TFT vs Golden Spatula

**Teamfight Tactics (TFT)** and **Golden Spatula** are closely related, but they are not exactly the same product.

## Teamfight Tactics

Teamfight Tactics is Riot Games' official auto-battler game. It is available through the League of Legends client on PC, and in some regions also through TFT Mobile.

## Golden Spatula

Golden Spatula, known in Chinese as **Jin Chanchan Zhi Zhan**, is the official Chinese mobile version related to TFT. It is published by Tencent for the Chinese mainland market.

## Relationship

The two games share the same core auto-battler foundation:

- buying champions from a shop
- building team compositions
- activating traits
- combining items
- fighting other players automatically

However, Golden Spatula may have different UI, events, modes, pacing, localization, and region-specific content.

## Simple Summary

TFT is the original global Teamfight Tactics game. Golden Spatula is the officially licensed Chinese mobile version with local adaptations.
