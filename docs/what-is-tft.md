# What Is Teamfight Tactics?

**Teamfight Tactics**, commonly shortened to **TFT**, is an auto-battler strategy game developed by Riot Games.

In TFT, players build a team of champions, place them on a board, and fight automatically against other players' teams. The main strategy comes from choosing champions, combining traits, managing gold, positioning units, and adapting to the items and shops offered during the game.

## Core Ideas

- **Champions**: Units that fight automatically on the board.
- **Traits**: Synergies activated by fielding champions that share the same trait.
- **Items**: Equipment that improves champion stats or adds special effects.
- **Gold**: The main resource used to buy champions, level up, and refresh the shop.
- **Positioning**: Placement of units on the board to protect carries and counter enemies.

## Common Abbreviations

- **TFT**: Teamfight Tactics
- **Comp**: Team composition
- **Carry**: Main damage-dealing champion
- **Tank**: Frontline champion that absorbs damage
- **Econ**: Economy management
- **Roll**: Refreshing the shop
